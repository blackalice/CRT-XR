## Easier to start
- First-run setup is four steps instead of six. Console names now sit beside the consoles found in your library, and videos and music are optional.
- Console names start from your time zone as well as your language, so a UK Steam Deck reporting American English now starts with PAL names.
- The Steam Deck installer says exactly what head tracking still needs (Decky Loader, XR Gaming or its driver) and can take you straight back to Gaming Mode.
## Windows glasses
- Set up VITURE head tracking by choosing the SDK's ZIP: no more copying DLLs into an sdk folder. Find it on the start-up screen or in View & glasses › Tracking.
- Open glasses view first, then switch the glasses to SBS when asked: the order no longer matters.
- CRT XR warns when SpaceWalker is holding the glasses' tracking.
## Staying up to date
- What's new (this page) appears once after each update, in Setups & help › Help.
- Setups & help › System › Updates can check GitHub for a newer release, once a day or when you choose Check now. It's off until you turn it on, and nothing is downloaded for you.
