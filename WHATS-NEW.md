## Looks you can share between games
- Picture › Looks › New look from this picture saves the picture as a named look. Give it to one game, a whole console, or a hand-picked set from your library with Choose games and consoles.
- Looks are linked, not copied: edit a look once and every game using it changes. A game's own tweaks still sit on top.
- Copy share code puts a look on the clipboard to share; Add a shared look adds one.
- Your old My looks slots became looks, with their names.
- Picture › Looks says which look the game is using, and Clear this game's own changes explains and undoes a game that looks different.
## Simpler settings
- Settings show the everyday options. Fine-tuning (signal detail, calibration, picture position, lamp placement) is a row away: select "advanced settings hidden", or turn on Show advanced settings in Menu & access › Accessibility. If you used CRT XR before, you still see everything.
- Saved setups are now Scenes: a look, a room and your view, saved together, in Scenes & help.
## Easier to start
- First-run setup is four steps instead of six. Console names now sit beside the consoles found in your library, and videos and music are optional.
- Console names start from your time zone as well as your language, so a UK Steam Deck reporting American English now starts with PAL names.
- The Steam Deck installer says exactly what head tracking still needs (Decky Loader, XR Gaming or its driver) and can take you straight back to Gaming Mode.
## Windows glasses
- Set up VITURE head tracking by choosing the SDK's ZIP: no more copying DLLs into an sdk folder. Find it on the start-up screen or in View & glasses › Tracking.
- Open glasses view first, then switch the glasses to SBS when asked: the order no longer matters.
- CRT XR warns when SpaceWalker is holding the glasses' tracking.
## Staying up to date
- What's new (this page) appears once after each update, in Scenes & help › Help.
- Scenes & help › System › Updates can check GitHub for a newer release, once a day or when you choose Check now. It's off until you turn it on, and nothing is downloaded for you.
