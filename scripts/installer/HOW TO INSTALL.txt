CRT XR for Steam Deck: how to install
=====================================

1. In Desktop Mode, double-click "Install CRT XR" in this folder.
   If you are asked, choose "Trust and Launch" (or allow it to run).

2. Follow the prompts. Steam closes for a moment while CRT XR is added to your
   library. Updating keeps your settings, saves and Steam shortcut.

3. Return to Gaming Mode. CRT XR is in your library under Non-Steam.

That's it. Keep this folder together until the install finishes, then you can
delete it. "CRT XR files" holds the app itself; you do not need to open it.


Head tracking
-------------
Needs Decky Loader with the XR Gaming plugin, using a version that supports your
glasses (VITURE Pro 2 currently needs the testing version). Without it, CRT XR
shows a fixed view. Leave XR Gaming's virtual display off.

Your games
----------
No games are included. In CRT XR, open Game library > Library locations and
choose your main ROM folder. An EmuDeck "Emulation/roms" folder works as it is.

Sega CD and Atari Lynx also need their BIOS files (bios_CD_U.bin and friends,
lynxboot.img). CRT XR finds them in the "bios" folder next to your roms folder
(the EmuDeck layout), or in ~/Applications/CRT-XR/bios.
