CRT XR for Windows: how to start
================================

1. Extract this whole ZIP to a folder, for example Documents\CRT XR.
   (Running it from inside the ZIP will not work.)

2. Double-click CRT-XR.exe.
   Windows may say it protected your PC, because CRT XR is not signed:
   choose "More info", then "Run anyway".

3. Choose "Play on this screen without glasses", or set up VITURE glasses
   with docs\WINDOWS-SETUP.md. Glasses tracking needs the VITURE SDK, which
   is not included.

There is nothing to install. Keep CRT-XR.exe together with the files and
folders next to it.


Your games
----------
No games are included. In CRT XR, open Game library > Library locations and
choose your main ROM folder (the one containing snes, nes, megadrive...).

Sega CD and Atari Lynx also need their BIOS files (bios_CD_U.bin and friends,
lynxboot.img). Put them in a "bios" folder next to CRT-XR.exe, or in the
"bios" folder next to your roms folder.

Updating
--------
Close CRT XR, then extract the new version to a new folder. Settings and saves
are kept separately, in %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\CRT XR.
